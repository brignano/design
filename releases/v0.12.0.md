The `A|B` logo is now part of the package, redrawn: the initials in Geist at 800 with the bar between them as a text cursor in the mark colour, plus an ink tile for favicons and app icons. This release also adds the filter chip.

## Upgrading: what changes on your site

Nothing changes on upgrade. No tokens or classes were removed. To use the new logo, switch your header, favicon and app icons to the files below.

## The logo

- **`@brignano/design/logo/paths.mjs`** exports `MARK` and `TILE` path data. Inline it so the letters take `--ink` and the bar takes `--mark-current`, and the header follows the site theme.
- **`logo/mark.svg`** is the mark for an `<img>`, inked for the OS theme.
- **`logo/tile.svg`**, **`logo/tile-180.png`** and **`logo/tile-512.png`** are the favicon, `apple-touch-icon` and manifest icon.
- The rules are in DESIGN.md §5, "The logo": the letters are ink and only the bar is amber, 24 to 32px tall in a header, the tile for icons only.

```tsx
import { MARK } from "@brignano/design/logo/paths.mjs";

<svg viewBox={MARK.viewBox} className="h-8 w-auto" role="img" aria-label="Anthony Brignano">
  <path d={MARK.letters} style={{ fill: "var(--ink)" }} />
  <path d={MARK.bar} style={{ fill: "var(--mark-current)" }} />
</svg>
```

## A filter chip

- **`.chip`** is a `<button>` with `aria-pressed` that toggles one filter on a list, and any number can be on. Off is the card ground with a control edge, and on is the `--i` fill. Shown on `previews/chips.html`.
- DESIGN.md §15 now says no emoji as icons or category markers. Use a word or a Lucide icon.
