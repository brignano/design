Product pages built to be understood by a stranger on a phone in five seconds, and each product now looks like itself.

## Product pages: show, then tell

`product-page.css` has a new hero. It splits into **head** (status, an audience line, a thesis of 8 words or fewer), **proof** and **body** (one-sentence subhead, actions, a short note). On a phone they stack in that order, so the product shows on the first screen.

- **Bands after the hero:**
  - **What you get** (`.pp-gets`): three cards that each lead with a picture.
  - **Comparison** (`.pp-compare`): the product beside the obvious alternative, in words.
  - **How it works** (`.pp-how`): three numbered steps.
  - The old four-chapter story is down to at most one chapter.
- **A browser window frame** (`.pp-browser`, with `.pp-browser-bar` and `.pp-browser-url`), alongside the existing phone frame.
- **`.pp-audience`:** a line saying who the product is for, above the thesis.

## Each product looks like itself

Before this, every product page was the same page with the words swapped. Each page now makes three choices:

- **A product mark.** Set `data-mark="teal"`, `"violet"` or `"magenta"` on `.pp`. New tokens: `--mark-teal`, `--mark-violet`, `--mark-magenta`. The mark colours the product's glyph, a short rule above the thesis, a faint stage behind the hero proof, and the step numerals. Buttons stay cobalt on every product.
- **A hero layout.** Set `data-layout` on `.pp-hero`:
  - `split` (default): the proof sits on the right.
  - `mirror`: the proof sits on the left.
  - `stage`: words and actions are centred, with the proof full width beneath them.
- **A proof frame** that matches where the product lives: bare, a phone, or a browser window.

## Check a page before it ships

`scripts/fold.mjs` now ships in the package. It opens a page at iPhone size and fails when:

- the thesis, the proof or the primary action is missing from the first screen;
- there are more than **40 words** of copy on the first screen, or more than **180** on the whole page;
- the page runs past 7 phone screens, or scrolls sideways.

```sh
node node_modules/@brignano/design/scripts/fold.mjs https://your.product/ --shots shots
```

DESIGN.md §13 is rewritten around the order of work. Each step comes from a new ADR, ADR-0010 or ADR-0011:

1. **Brief.** Write five lines in `BRIEF.md`: Who, Pain, Promise, Moment, Ask.
2. **Page.** Build the page from the brief.
3. **`fold.mjs`.** Run the check above.
4. **Stranger test.** Run the fixed prompt from §13 and compare the answers to the brief.

## Changed

- `.pp-audience` is now ink, no longer cobalt. Cobalt means "you can act on this".
- `.pp-subhead` should read as the pain, then the fix.
- The header no longer repeats the status.
- The current clip step's bar takes the product mark rather than ink.
- The how-it-works numerals take the product mark.
