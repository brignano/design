A product page's header nav links are now easy to tap on a touch screen.

## Upgrading: what changes on your site

- **On a touch screen, the header's in-page nav links on a product page are 44px tall**, like its brand link, footer links and buttons since 0.10.0. They show above 860px wide, so this mostly reaches tablets. With a mouse nothing changes, and the header keeps its height.

Nothing else changes. No tokens, classes or markup were added or removed.
