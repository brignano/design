A card that stands for a product can now put that product's picture on a faint field of its colour, the same tint as the stage on its product page, so a product looks the same in a list as on its own page.

## Upgrading: what changes on your site

Nothing changes on upgrade. No tokens or classes were removed, and existing cards look as before.

## A product's card can carry its stage

- **`.card-media.card-stage`** tints the picture's ground with an 8% mix of the product's mark, the same strength as `--pp-stage` on a product page. Put `data-mark="teal"` (or `violet`, `magenta`) on the card or a section around it. A product with no product page has no `data-mark` and gets the family amber.
- **The picture is shown whole.** On a stage it's padded and contained in its 16:9 box instead of cropped to fill, which suits drawings.
- **Only for a card that stands for a product**, and only as its picture's ground (DESIGN.md §3, ADR-0018). Titles, links, pills and buttons on the card stay as they are, and a trip or post card never gets a stage.

```html
<article class="card card-link" data-mark="teal">
  <div class="card-media card-stage"><svg viewBox="0 0 320 180" aria-hidden="true">…</svg></div>
  <header class="card-header"><h3 class="card-title"><a href="/projects/driftwood">Driftwood</a></h3></header>
</article>
```

If your site copied the 8% tint locally, switch to `.card-stage` and drop the copy.

`npm run check` now fails if the card stage and the product-page stage drift apart.
