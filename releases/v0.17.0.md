The logo's cursor can blink, like a text cursor, when a page loads.

## Upgrading: what changes on your site

- **New `.cursor-blink` class.** Put it on the inlined logo's cursor path (`<path d={MARK.bar} className="cursor-blink" …>`), or on a `.stamp`. The cursor blinks three times on load, then stays solid. It doesn't move under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Nothing changes until you add the class.

## Also

- ADR-0022: three blinks on load replaces "at most one blink" in DESIGN.md §5. The tab icon never blinks.
