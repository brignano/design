Use it

<!-- In the page from the start, both lists empty -->
<section class="toast-region" aria-label="Notifications">
  <div class="toast-list" role="status"></div>
  <div class="toast-list" role="alert"></div>
</section>

<!-- What your script adds to a list -->
<div class="toast toast-success">
  <svg aria-hidden="true" …></svg>
  <div class="toast-content">
    <p class="toast-title">Trip saved</p>
    <p class="toast-body">Lisbon, 4 days is in your list.</p>
  </div>
  <button class="btn btn-ghost btn-sm toast-action">Undo</button>
  <button class="btn btn-ghost btn-icon btn-sm toast-close" aria-label="Dismiss">…</button>
</div>

Use when

  • An action finished somewhere the reader isn't looking, such as "Trip saved".
  • The reader may want to undo what just happened: add a .toast-action.
  • A background task failed or needs attention, and the reader can carry on meanwhile.
  • For the words, follow Success messages in the content guidelines.

Don't use when

  • News that needs nothing from anyone: no info toast, put a .msg on the page instead.
  • A modal dialog is open: report inside the dialog with a .msg.
  • An error on one field: .field-error under the field.

Kinds

  • .toast-success, .toast-attention, .toast-danger. The title says in words what happened; the icon and edge repeat it. No info toast: put a .msg on the page instead.

Behaviour your script owns

  • The region. One per page, in the HTML from the start with both lists empty. Newest goes at the bottom.
  • Timing. Remove a success toast after 5 seconds, and pause that timer while the pointer is on it or focus is inside it. Attention and danger toasts stay until dismissed, because they ask for something.
  • Leaving. Set data-leaving on the toast, then remove it on its own transitionend (the buttons' hover transitions bubble up too), or after 200ms, whichever comes first.
  • Not over a dialog. While a modal dialog is open, everything outside it is inert, toasts included. Report inside the dialog with a .msg instead.
  • No script in design. The queue and timers are yours. The one this page runs is below.

Accessibility

  • Live regions. Success and attention toasts go in the role="status" list, announced without interrupting. Danger toasts go in the role="alert" list, which interrupts and sits below. Two separate live regions, never one inside the other: screen readers handle nested ones unreliably.
  • Focus. If focus is inside a toast when it leaves, move it to the next toast, or back to where it was before the toast took it. Never let it drop to the page.
  • Reduced motion. The transition still runs, in 0.01ms, so transitionend still fires.
  • The icon is aria-hidden, and the close button needs an aria-label such as "Dismiss".
