Use

<!-- In the page from the start, empty -->
<div class="toast-region" role="status" aria-label="Notifications"></div>

<!-- What your script adds -->
<div class="toast toast-success">
  <svg aria-hidden="true" …></svg>
  <div class="toast-content">
    <p class="toast-title">Trip saved</p>
    <p class="toast-body">Lisbon, 4 days is in your list.</p>
  </div>
  <button class="btn btn-ghost btn-sm toast-action">Undo</button>
  <button class="btn btn-ghost btn-icon btn-sm toast-close" aria-label="Dismiss">…</button>
</div>
  • Kinds. .toast-success, .toast-attention, .toast-danger. The title says in words what happened; the icon and edge repeat it. No info toast: put a .msg on the page instead.
  • The region. One per page, in the HTML from the start and empty, with role="status" so screen readers announce what's added without interrupting. A danger toast also carries role="alert", which interrupts. Newest goes at the bottom.
  • Timing. Remove a success toast after 5 seconds, and pause that timer while the pointer is on it or focus is inside it. Attention and danger toasts stay until dismissed, because they ask for something.
  • Leaving. Set data-leaving on the toast, then remove it on transitionend (or after 200ms, whichever comes first). With reduced motion the transition still runs, in 0.01ms, so transitionend still fires.
  • Over a dialog. A modal dialog sits in the top layer, above any z-index. To show toasts over one, give the region popover="manual" and call showPopover() once.
  • No script in design. The queue and timers are yours. The one this page runs is below.
