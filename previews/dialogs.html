Dialog and sheet

Native <dialog> with showModal(): the browser traps focus, Esc closes it and focus goes back to the button that opened it. A sheet comes in from the right on a wide screen and from the bottom on a phone.

Use

<button class="btn" commandfor="rename" command="show-modal">Rename</button>

<dialog class="dialog" id="rename" aria-labelledby="rename-title">
  <div class="dialog-header">
    <h2 class="dialog-title" id="rename-title">Rename trip</h2>
    <form method="dialog">
      <button class="btn btn-ghost btn-icon btn-sm dialog-close" aria-label="Close">…</button>
    </form>
  </div>
  <div class="dialog-body">…</div>
  <div class="dialog-footer">
    <form method="dialog"><button class="btn">Cancel</button></form>
    <button class="btn btn-primary">Save name</button>
  </div>
</dialog>
  • Open with dialog.showModal(). Where the browser supports commandfor and command="show-modal", a button opens it with no script; this page adds a three-line fallback for browsers that don't.
  • Close with a button inside <form method="dialog"> or with Esc. The close button in the header is always there.
  • A confirm names the action on its destructive button (“Delete trip”), never “OK”, and puts autofocus on the safe choice.
  • Never open a dialog on top of a dialog. Close the first, or put the second step inside it.
  • Scroll locking is what showModal() gives: the page behind can't be interacted with. Locking the page's scroll as well is up to you.
