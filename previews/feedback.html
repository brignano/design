Use it

<div class="msg msg-danger" role="alert">
  <svg aria-hidden="true" …></svg>
  <div class="msg-content">
    <p class="msg-title">Couldn't reach WakaTime</p>
    <p class="msg-body">Showing the last cached week.</p>
    <div class="msg-actions"><button class="btn btn-sm">Try again</button></div>
  </div>
  <button class="btn btn-ghost btn-icon btn-sm msg-close" aria-label="Dismiss"><svg …></svg></button>
</div>

<!-- above the site header -->
<div class="banner banner-attention"><svg …></svg><div class="msg-content">…</div></div>

Kinds

  • Info: news that needs nothing from anyone. Neutral, with a cobalt icon.
  • Success: something finished the way it should.
  • Attention: something will go wrong unless someone acts.
  • Danger: something failed or was lost. Say what happens next.

Parts

  • An icon slot for any inline SVG, then .msg-title, .msg-body and optional .msg-actions.
  • .msg-close is a small ghost .btn-icon; your script removes the message.
  • .banner is the same parts in a full-width strip above the site header. A bare .msg or .banner looks like the info kind.

Accessibility

  • role="status" for info, success and attention: read when the reader is free.
  • role="alert" for danger, and for attention that needs action now: read at once.
  • The role must be on the page before the text arrives, or nothing is announced. Insert the text into a live region that already exists.
  • The icon is aria-hidden; the title says what happened in words.

When not to

  • A short-lived confirmation after an action: a toast.
  • An error on one field: .field-error under the field.
  • More than one banner at a time. Show the most urgent.
