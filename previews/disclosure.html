Disclosure and accordion

Show more on demand. Native <details>, no script; the chevron turns when it opens.

Light

On its own

What happens to my photos after the trip?

They stay in your album until you delete them. Nothing is shared unless you send the link.

Accordion, one open at a time

Can I change the dates?

Yes, up to 48 hours before the first booking. Bookings already paid move with the trip.

Who can see the itinerary?

Only people you invite. Each invite can be view-only or can edit.

Does it work offline?

The itinerary and confirmations are saved on your phone once opened.

Use it

<details class="disclosure">
  <summary>What happens to my photos?</summary>
  <p>They stay in your album until you delete them.</p>
</details>

<!-- An accordion: one open at a time, because they share a name -->
<div class="accordion">
  <details class="disclosure" name="faq" open>…</details>
  <details class="disclosure" name="faq">…</details>
</div>

Use when

  • FAQs on a product page.
  • Detail most people skip, like advanced settings or the full log behind a summary.

Don't use when

  • The content is what people came for. Show it.
  • People switch between views of one thing. Use tabs.
  • It hides an error or a required field.

Behaviour

  • The browser does it all: click, Enter or Space on the summary opens and closes it, and find-in-page opens a closed one when it matches.
  • Give every <details> in a group the same name to allow only one open at a time. Leave name off to let several stay open.
  • Add open to start one expanded. The height doesn't animate; the chevron turns, and stops turning with reduced motion.

Accessibility

  • The summary is the button: keep it short, and don't put links or other controls inside it.
  • Screen readers announce expanded and collapsed from <details> itself. Don't add aria-expanded.
  • The open state shows by the content appearing and the chevron, not colour.
