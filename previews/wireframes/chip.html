Wireframe: filter chip

For issue 167. A .chip is a toggle button for a filterable list: tap one to turn a filter on, tap again to turn it off, and any number can be on at once. Off is the card ground with a control edge; on is the interactive fill, the same as a chosen segmented option. The chips below work: try them.

Rejected:

  • A wash for the on state: reads weaker than the segmented control beside it (already decided in the issue).
  • A tick inside the on chip: the chip changes width when toggled, so the row jumps.
  • A fully round pill shape: every other control uses the control radius, and round reads as a status pill.
  • One sideways-scrolling row on a phone: hides which filters exist. Chips wrap instead.
  • Checkboxes drawn as chips: a filter that applies at once is a button with a pressed state, not a form field.
