Identity: three directions to choose from

Issue #137. The trail is gone from brignano.io, and nothing else that makes a page yours ships in the package, so the apps look anonymous. Each direction below is drawn on the same three surfaces: a marketing hero, an app screen and an error page, using Driftwood as the example. Switch your device between light and dark to see both themes.

My recommendation: 1 and 2 together, with 3b for the big moments. They don't compete: 1 is the voice every screen shares, 2 tells the products apart, and 3b gives error codes and the family stamp weight without loading another font. In all of them the buttons stay cobalt and the state colours keep their meaning.

Rejected: bringing back the trail (removed for hurting the experience); a brand colour on buttons or links (breaks "one hue, one meaning"); illustrations or a mascot (DESIGN.md §5).

1. Mono voice

Every screen opens with a small uppercase mono label, and every number, date and code is set in mono. The A|B stamp sits in the footer. Quiet, costs nothing, and it is already how your best pages look.

2. Product mark

Each product keeps its own colour (teal here) on its glyph and its rule, on the marketing page, inside the app and on the error page. It never touches a button or a link. This is what tells Driftwood from homelab at a glance.

3a. Display face: Silkscreen

The pixel face you have today, used for labels, the error code and the stamp. Distinctive, but it reads retro and gets hard to read below about 12px.

3b. Display face: heavy Geist

The same moments set in Geist at its heaviest and tightest. Modern, no extra font to load, and it stays in the family with everything else on the page.
