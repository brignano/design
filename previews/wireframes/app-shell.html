What to look at

  • A1 widths: 15rem open, 3.5rem as a rail. Collapse with « at the bottom of the sidebar.
  • A2 collapsed: icons stay and labels hide (still read out). The proposal adds a tooltip with the label on each icon.
  • A3 detail: select a host. From 75rem it's a third column. Between 48 and 75rem it's a sheet from the right over the list. On a phone it's a sheet from the bottom.
  • A4 phone: at 375 the sidebar goes away and ☰ opens it as a sheet from the left. Esc or tapping outside closes it.
  • Every width switch is a container query on the shell, so dragging the frame is the same as resizing the window.
