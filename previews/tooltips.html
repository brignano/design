  <button class="btn btn-ghost btn-icon" interestfor="tip-share" aria-labelledby="tip-share">…</button>

  • Names only. One short phrase that names what the button does. A sentence, a link or anything the reader must know goes in a popover or on the page.
  • The name comes from the tooltip. aria-labelledby on the trigger points at the tooltip, so screen readers hear the same words people see, with or without hover.
  • Where it opens. With interestfor support, the browser opens it as a hint popover above the trigger (below if there's no room) and Esc dismisses it. Elsewhere, .tooltip-wrap shows it on hover and focus. No script either way.
  • Timing. 300ms in, nothing out. With reduced motion there's no fade, but the delay stays.
