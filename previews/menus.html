Popover and menu

The native popover attribute: Esc or a click outside closes it, and it opens below the button that opened it with CSS anchor positioning, flipping when there's no room. A .menu inside it lists actions or links. Opening and closing take 150ms, and are instant with reduced motion (ADR-0013).

Use

<button class="btn btn-icon" popovertarget="trip-menu" aria-label="Trip actions">…</button>

<div class="popover" id="trip-menu" popover>
  <div class="menu">
    <button class="menu-item">Rename <span class="menu-item-hint">R</span></button>
    <a class="menu-item" href="/trips/lisbon">Open itinerary</a>
    <hr class="menu-separator">
    <button class="menu-item menu-item-danger">Delete trip</button>
  </div>
</div>
  • Opening. popovertarget on a button opens it with no script, and that button becomes its anchor. Opened any other way, give the opener anchor-name: --trip and the popover position-anchor: --trip.
  • Items. Each item is a <button> or an <a>, never a <div>. The destructive action goes last, after a separator, with .menu-item-danger. A disabled item says why in its hint.
  • ARIA contract. With no script, leave roles off: the items are buttons and links, Tab moves through them, and Esc closes the popover. Add role="menu" on .menu and role="menuitem" on each item only when your script also does the rest of the menu pattern: focus moves to the first item on open, Up and Down arrows move between items (wrapping), Home and End jump to the ends, and Tab closes the menu. Give the opener aria-haspopup="menu" only then, too.
  • Other content. A popover can hold anything short, like the map layers above. Give it aria-labelledby pointing at its heading. Anything longer, or that needs a Save button, is a dialog.
  • Without anchor positioning. The browser centres the popover. To keep it under the opener, set two custom properties when it opens: 
    pop.addEventListener('toggle', (e) => {
  if (e.newState !== 'open' || CSS.supports('position-area: block-end')) return;
  const r = opener.getBoundingClientRect();
  pop.style.setProperty('--popover-top', r.bottom + 'px');
  pop.style.setProperty('--popover-left', r.left + 'px');
});
