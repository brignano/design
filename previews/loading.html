Loading and empty states

A spinner when you don't know the layout yet, a skeleton when you do. Progress and meter are the native elements, drawn from tokens. An empty state says what will be here and offers one way to fill it. With reduced motion the spinner and shimmer hold still.

Use when

  • Spinner or skeleton. A skeleton only when you know the layout that's coming; otherwise a spinner.
  • Progress for how far a task has got; meter for a level in a known range.
  • Empty state when a list or view has nothing in it yet.
  • For the words, follow Empty states in the content guidelines.

Don't use when

  • The wait is on a button the reader just pressed: use aria-busy="true" on the button.
  • You don't know the layout that's coming: use a spinner, not a skeleton.
  • An amount you can't measure: leave out value on the progress bar rather than guess.
  • A count that is the whole point of the view: use a stat tile, not a meter.

Progress and meter

  • Progress is how far a task has got; leave out value when you can't say, and it shows a pulsing segment in the middle, which can't be read as an amount.
  • Meter is a level in a known range: its fill is cobalt in the optimum range, attention one range away and danger two away.
  • Without the class they stay native.

Empty states

  • One line saying what will be here, at most one action, an icon and no illustration.
  • If there's nothing the reader can do, leave the button out.

Accessibility

  • A spinner always has words beside it, or role="img" aria-label="Loading" where there's no room.
  • Busy regions. Put aria-busy="true" on the container that's loading and remove it when the content lands. Skeleton blocks are aria-hidden.
  • Always label progress and meter with a <label for>, and put the value in words inside the element too.
  • Reduced motion. The spinner shows a still arc, the shimmer stops and leaves a plain block, and an indeterminate bar holds still as its centre segment. The words and aria-busy carry the meaning.
  • Under forced colours the spinner, progress and meter draw in system colours.
