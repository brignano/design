Use it

<article class="card card-link">
  <div class="card-media"><img src="lisbon.jpg" alt=""></div>
  <header class="card-header">
    <h3 class="card-title"><a href="/trips/lisbon">Lisbon, 4 days</a></h3>
    <p class="card-meta">12–15 Mar · 2 people</p>
  </header>
  <div class="card-body"><p>…</p></div>
  <footer class="card-footer"><span class="pill pill-success">Booked</span></footer>
</article>

Parts

  • .card-header holds .card-title and .card-meta; .card-body and .card-footer follow. Every part is optional.
  • .card-media goes first and runs edge to edge at 16:9.
  • The footer sits at the bottom, so cards in a row line up.
  • Radius --radius; the grid of cards is the layout's job.

Link cards

  • .card-link stretches the title link over the card with ::after. Don't wrap the card in an <a>: screen readers would read the whole card as the link name.
  • Hover fills with --surface-hover and underlines the title.
  • Buttons and links inside still work on their own.

Accessibility

  • The link's name is the title, so it says where it goes.
  • Focus shows once, round the whole card.
  • Decorative media takes alt=""; media that carries meaning gets real alt text.

When not to

  • A card inside a card: use a divider or a list instead.
  • A single fact: use a stat tile or a key-value list.
  • A whole page section: use the layout primitives.
