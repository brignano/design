Use it

<article class="card card-link">
  <div class="card-media"><img src="lisbon.jpg" alt=""></div>
  <header class="card-header">
    <h3 class="card-title"><a href="/trips/lisbon">Lisbon, 4 days</a></h3>
    <p class="card-meta">12–15 Mar · 2 people</p>
  </header>
  <div class="card-body"><p>…</p></div>
  <footer class="card-footer"><span class="pill pill-success">Booked</span></footer>
</article>

Use when

  • A list of things of the same kind, each with a title and a few details, like trips or posts.
  • Each item leads to its own page: add .card-link.
  • Items sit in a grid and should line up, footers included.

Don't use when

  • A card inside a card: use a divider or a list instead.
  • A single fact: use a stat tile or a key-value list.
  • A whole page section: use the layout primitives.

Parts

  • .card-header holds .card-title and .card-meta; .card-body and .card-footer follow. Every part is optional.
  • .card-media goes first and runs edge to edge at 16:9, cropped to fill.
  • .card-media.card-stage is for a drawing of a product: the drawing is padded and contained, never cropped, on a faint field of the product's mark.
  • The footer sits at the bottom, so cards in a row line up.
  • Radius --radius-card; the grid of cards is the layout's job.

Link cards

  • .card-link stretches the title link over the card with ::after. Don't wrap the card in an <a>: screen readers would read the whole card as the link name.
  • Hover fills with --surface-hover and underlines the title.
  • Buttons and links inside still work on their own.

Product cards on a stage

  • Only on a card that stands for a product, behind that product's picture (DESIGN.md §3, ADR-0018). Not for trips, posts or anything else.
  • Put data-mark on the card (or a section around it). The field is the same 8% mix as the hero stage on the product's page, so the two match.
  • A product without a product page has no data-mark and stays on the family amber.
  • The mark is the picture's ground only. The title, links, pills and buttons on the card stay as they are.
<article class="card card-link" data-mark="teal">
  <div class="card-media card-stage"><svg viewBox="0 0 320 180" aria-hidden="true">…</svg></div>
  <header class="card-header"><h3 class="card-title"><a href="/projects/driftwood">Driftwood</a></h3></header>
</article>

Accessibility

  • The link's name is the title, so it says where it goes.
  • Focus shows once, round the whole card.
  • Decorative media takes alt=""; media that carries meaning gets real alt text.
  • The stage is a background, so forced colours drop it; the drawing and the card's border stay.
