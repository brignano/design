Content guidelines

How a brignano product words things: one rule, one do and one don't each. English only for now; marketing copy follows the product page brief.

Voice

Competent, calm, plain words: what happened and what to do next. No hype, no jokes, no apology theatre.

Do

Your changes are saved.

Don't

Awesome! Your changes have been successfully saved!

Sentence case

Capitalise the first word and proper nouns, everywhere: buttons, headings, labels, tabs, menus, titles. An eyebrow may be uppercase through CSS, but it is typed in sentence case.

Do

Don't

Buttons and links

A button is a verb and its object, so it reads on its own. A link names where it goes.

Do

Read the release notes

Don't

For the notes, click here.

Form labels

A short noun phrase above the field, never a placeholder. Mark the optional fields, not the required ones; formats go in the hint.

Do

We only call about bookings.

Don't

Errors

What happened, then how to fix it, next to what went wrong. Name the field or the action, never the person.

Do

That date has passed. Pick today or later.

Don't

You entered a wrong date.

Empty states

Say what will be here and give the one action that fills it. If the person can't fill it, say why it's empty.

Do

No trips yet

Plan one and it shows up here.

Don't

Nothing to see here!

Confirmations

Ask only before something that can't be undone. The title names the action and the object, the body says what goes with it, the buttons are the verb and a way out.

Do

Delete Lisbon 2027?

Its 12 photos go with it. This can't be undone.

Don't

Are you sure?

Success messages

Past tense, the object first, and Undo when there is one. Quiet: a toast or an inline line, never a dialog.

Do

Trip deleted

Don't

Success!

Dates

Day, short month, year in the interface. ISO 8601 in mono for data a person copies, sorts or searches.

Do

Departs Sat 7 Nov 2026

2026-11-07T09:30Z deploy ok

Don't

Departs 11/07/2026

Departs November 7th, 2026

Times

Twelve-hour, a space, capital AM or PM; drop ":00" on the hour in prose. Add the zone when the reader might be elsewhere. No 24-hour time.

Do

9:30 AM · 3 PM ET · 2–3 AM UTC

Don't

21:30 · 9:30am

Numbers

Numerals always, a comma every three digits, a space before a unit except %.

Do

3 trips · 10,482 photos · 64 GB · 64%

Don't

three trips · 10482 photos · 64GB · 64 %

Currency

The symbol first with no space. Whole amounts drop ".00" in prose; a price table shows two decimals on every row. Name the currency only when it could be mistaken.

Do

$1,240 · $12.50

Don't

$1240.00 · 12.5 USD

What never appears

Exclamation marks, "simply", "just" or "easy", "Oops", emoji as words, typed ALL CAPS, and jargon on marketing pages.

Do

Couldn't load your trips. Try again.

Don't

Oops! Something went wrong!! Simply refresh.
