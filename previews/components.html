Components

Shared controls and patterns, shipped as plain CSS. One import, any framework.

Use it

@import '@brignano/design/tokens.css';
@import '@brignano/design/components.css';

The rules every component follows

  • One cascade layer. Everything sits in @layer brignano.components, so your own CSS and Tailwind utilities win without !important.
  • Short names. .btn, .btn-primary. State comes from real attributes like disabled, aria-invalid and [open], never from a class.
  • Native first. Dialogs are <dialog>, popovers use the popover attribute, and disclosures are <details>. When a component needs JavaScript, its page documents the contract and your app owns the script.
  • Tokens only. No hex values. Contrast is checked in both themes on every change.
